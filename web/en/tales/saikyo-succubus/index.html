Still able to take a high-minded cleric who had given his life to his god and rot him, over days and days of standing guard, into an addict masturbating over the succubus in the crystal coffin. Lilista understood exactly how terrible her existence was, as a force acting on men.

And she was right: the instant he saw that jealous, resentful look on his teacher’s face, the hero had settled on the plan for the coming year. He believed, instinctively, that the life he had dreamed of could be made real through this woman’s hands.

“So~ looking down at your own teacher’s corpse at your feet, you realised — it was this sleeping succubus who turned the hero’s companion into an enemy who’d lost his reason. And if that’s so, then waking her up ought to turn a great many more people against him too~♡”

“Ah, Lord Hero, you really are awful♡ Using me to corrupt your own people, just to stock up on new enemies♡ Or is it that the process itself gets that twisted little heart of yours going~?”

“Rest assured. You are the benefactor who made me what I am♡ Whatever Lord Hero needs… I’ll do everything in my power to provide~♡”

With that, Lilista stood, taking back the finger she had pressed to his lips.

She snapped her fingers in the air, and magic began to gather at her fingertip, twisting and churning and swelling until it became a mass of rose-coloured mist about the size of a mask.

“You’ve seen one of these, haven’t you~? The dream art that manufactures thirty minutes of heaven♡ Unlike Mother’s, though, mine can be cast instantly or condensed into a bundle like this… All Lord Hero has to do is carry it back to the royal castle and throw it into the face of the first person who comes hurrying out to greet you~♡”

“You won’t get to see the thirty minutes of filth and abandon inside, unfortunately~♡ But I promise you that when the thirty minutes are up and they’re let out of the dream… they will have become exactly what Lord Hero needs♡”

“Your ambitious wife, princess of the realm, who works herself to the bone over affairs of state~♡ That kind, gentle old king who’s spent his reign trying to keep every faction balanced~♡ Those proper, well-mannered courtiers who put your child’s education above everything else~♡ Every last one of them, thirty minutes later… becomes an ugly-hearted, ugly-faced villain piece, perfectly suited to being the object of revenge~ hehe♡”

“Fighting scum like that, who’ll stop at nothing — that’ll be thrilling and completely free of guilt. The perfect sequel to an adventure tale~♡ From here on, let’s each take what we want from the other, and have a lo~~~ng partnership♡ Lord♡ He♡ ro♡?”

Like royalty knighting a retainer, Lilista set that lump of magic and its evil curse lightly on the hero’s left shoulder.

His lips had been free for some time. He did not answer.

He only stayed where he was, kneeling in complete and willing agreement, and leaned forward to kiss the toe of Lilista’s boot in gratitude.
